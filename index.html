The Problem

Support has been too slow, too costly, and too hard to find

Costly screening

Too few specialists

A shortage of certified dyslexia teachers leaves families without the qualified support their children need.

Emotional toll

Untreated dyslexia can lead to anxiety, low self-esteem, and higher dropout rates over time.

Proven Method

Orton-Gillingham

Orton-Gillingham is widely regarded as a gold-standard approach because it provides explicit, systematic, and multisensory instruction tailored to how children with dyslexia learn. Its structured, cumulative method helps students build strong connections between sounds, letters, words, reading, and spelling.

FAQs

Questions parents ask before reaching out

What ages is Dyslexia Pal for?
Dyslexia Pal is designed for children who are learning to read, roughly ages 5 to 12 (kindergarten through sixth grade). The program adapts to each child's level, so it meets an early reader and an older child who is still struggling right where they are.
How do I know if my child has dyslexia?
Common signs include trouble sounding out words, slow or effortful reading, frequent spelling mistakes, mixing up similar letters, avoiding reading, and a family history of reading difficulty. Dyslexia affects about 1 in 5 children, yet most are never identified. Our free AI screening listens as your child reads and speaks and flags risk factors in minutes, right at home, so you can act early.
Is Dyslexia Pal based on real science?
Yes. Dyslexia Pal is built on the Science of Reading, the same body of research behind structured literacy and the Orton-Gillingham approach. It teaches reading in a systematic, step-by-step way, focusing on the phonics and decoding skills that dyslexic readers need to build accurate, automatic reading.
Does my child need a certified tutor?
No. Certified dyslexia specialists are in short supply and often expensive or booked out for months. Dyslexia Pal's Virtual Tutor listens as your child reads aloud using speech recognition and gives real-time feedback, so your child gets structured, personalized practice at home without a waitlist or a certified tutor.
How often should my child use it?
Short, consistent sessions work best. Even 10 to 15 minutes a few times a week builds the repetition dyslexic readers need. Dyslexia Pal creates a personalized plan and adapts as your child grows, and you can check progress anytime on the parent dashboard.
What does a typical session look like?
Sessions feel like play, not homework. Your child moves through story adventures and mini-games, reads aloud while the Virtual Tutor listens and guides them, and earns stars and badges along the way. Most sessions are short and end on a win, so kids build confidence right alongside their skills.
How much does Dyslexia Pal cost?
Dyslexia Pal uses simple, affordable subscription pricing, a small fraction of the cost of a private assessment (which can run up to $5,000) or ongoing private tutoring. You can start with the free screening, and monthly, annual, and lifetime plans will be available at launch.
